About Me

My Goal

My goal is to become a responsible and successful tourism professional in Bhutan by gaining knowledge about our culture, nature, and tourism industry, developing good communication and hospitality skills, and providing meaningful experiences to visitors while helping to promote and protect Bhutan’s unique heritage and environment.
