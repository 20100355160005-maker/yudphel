Welcome to Bhutan

Bhutan is a beautiful Himalayan kingdom known for its stunning mountains, peaceful valleys, rich culture, and unique traditions. It is a country where ancient customs and modern life exist together.

Bhutan attracts tourists with its beautiful landscapes, traditional festivals, unique culture, and delicious food. Popular places include Paro Taktsang, Punakha Dzong, Thimphu, and Phobjikha Valley.
